# Club Treasurer Guide: Getting Your Club Paid

This guide is for the person who looks after your club's money. It walks you through connecting
your club's bank account to myK9Show, one time, so your club is paid automatically after each show.

It takes about 10 minutes if you have the four things in the checklist below in front of you.

---

## How it works, in one minute

1. Exhibitors enter your show online and pay by card.
2. myK9Show holds the entry fees safely until your show is over.
3. Three days after your show's last day, your club's entry fees are sent to your club's bank
   account. You do not have to do anything to make that happen.

**Your club keeps the full entry fee.** Exhibitors pay a separate service fee on top of the entry
fee when they check out. That service fee covers card processing and myK9Show's costs. Nothing is
taken out of your club's entry fees, and your club pays nothing to myK9Show or to the card company.

Card payments are handled by a company called **Stripe**. Stripe is one of the largest payment
companies in the world. It is the company that actually moves the money into your club's bank
account, so it is Stripe that asks you the questions in this guide.

## Before you start: have these four things ready

Gather these first. Having them in front of you is what makes this a 10-minute job.

| What you need                                                                                              | Where to find it                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Your club's legal name and tax ID number (EIN)**                                                      | On the letter the IRS sent when the club got its EIN, or on last year's tax filing. The EIN is nine digits: 12-3456789. |
| **2. Your club's mailing address**                                                                         | The address the club uses on its tax paperwork.                                                                         |
| **3. One of the club's checks**                                                                            | The routing number and account number are printed along the bottom. Use the **club's** account, not a personal one.     |
| **4. Your own name, date of birth, home address, and the last four digits of your Social Security number** | You know these. See "Why does Stripe ask about me?" below.                                                              |

You also need:

- **A mobile phone** that can receive a text message. Stripe texts you a code to confirm it is you.
- **An email address you will keep.** Stripe sends deposit notices there.
- **To be signed in to myK9Show as an admin of your club.** If you do not see a **Payments** tab on
  your club's page in step 1 below, you are not set up as a club admin yet. Email the address at
  the end of this guide and we will fix that.

### The one thing that causes most delays

**Type the club's legal name exactly as the IRS has it.** Not the name on your website, and not a
shortened version. If the IRS letter says "Heartland Scent Work Club Inc", type exactly that,
including "Inc". When the name and the EIN do not match the IRS's records, Stripe cannot verify the
club and asks for more paperwork.

If you cannot find the IRS letter, the club's last tax filing shows the same name.

### Why does Stripe ask about me?

Federal banking law requires every payment company to confirm that a real person is responsible
for each account that receives money. That is all it is for.

- Stripe asks for your details because you are the person setting up the account.
- **myK9Show never sees or stores your date of birth, home address or Social Security number.**
  You type them on Stripe's own secure pages.
- It is not a credit check and does not affect your credit.
- The money still goes to the club's bank account, not to you.

### If your club does not have an EIN

Some small clubs have never applied for one. You can still be paid: in step 3 you would choose
"Individual" and use your own Social Security number, and deposits would go to whichever bank
account you enter. Most clubs prefer to keep club money under the club's own tax ID. Getting an EIN
is free and takes a few minutes on the IRS website (search for "apply for an EIN online"). We
recommend doing that first.

## Step by step

### Step 1. Open your club's Payments tab in myK9Show

1. Sign in to myK9Show.
2. Choose **Clubs** and open your club.
3. Choose the **Payments** tab.

You will see a box titled **Bank account** that says "No bank account is connected yet, so your
club can't receive entry fees."

### Step 2. Start the connection

1. Choose **Connect bank account**.
2. myK9Show shows the same checklist as above. When you have everything, choose
   **Continue to Stripe**.
3. myK9Show may ask you to type your myK9Show password again. This is a safety check, because you
   are about to choose where the club's money goes.

Your browser now leaves myK9Show and opens Stripe's pages. The address at the top of the screen
will show **stripe.com**. That is expected.

### Step 3. Answer Stripe's questions

Stripe asks its questions over several screens. Stripe updates the wording of its screens from
time to time, so yours may read a little differently, but the questions are the same. Here they
are in the order Stripe usually asks, with what to answer.

**Your email and mobile number.** Enter them. Stripe texts a six-digit code to your phone. Type it
in.

**Type of business.** Choose **Nonprofit organization**.

- If Stripe then asks for the structure, choose **Unincorporated non-profit** if your club is an
  association that has never filed incorporation papers with your state, or
  **Incorporated non-profit** if it has. If you are not sure, your club's secretary or the IRS
  letter will tell you. Either choice works for being paid.

**About the club.** Stripe asks for:

- **Legal business name:** the club's name exactly as the IRS has it.
- **Employer Identification Number (EIN):** the nine digits.
- **Business address** and **phone number:** the club's mailing address and a club phone, or yours.
- **Industry:** choose the closest match. "Membership organizations" or "Charities and social
  service organizations" are both fine.
- **Website or description:** enter your club's website. If the club has none, choose the option to
  describe the business instead and type: _Dog club that holds scent work trials. Entry fees are
  collected through myK9Show._

**About you.** Stripe calls you the "representative". Enter:

- your legal name, as on your driver's license
- your job title: **Treasurer** (or your actual office in the club)
- your date of birth
- your home address
- your phone number
- the last four digits of your Social Security number

If Stripe asks whether you own 25% or more of the business, the answer for a club is **No**. If it
asks you to confirm that you have significant responsibility for managing the organization, the
answer is **Yes**: that is what a treasurer is.

**Where the money should go.** This is the club's bank account.

- Stripe may offer to connect to your bank by signing in to it. You can do that, or choose
  **Enter bank details manually**, which is simpler with a check in front of you.
- Type the **routing number** (nine digits, bottom left of the check) and the **account number**
  (to the right of it).
- Use the club's checking account.

**Review.** Stripe shows everything you entered. Read the club's name, EIN and bank account
numbers once more, then choose **Agree and submit**.

### Step 4. Come back to myK9Show

Stripe sends you back to the Payments tab automatically. You will see one of these:

| What the Bank account box says               | What it means                                                                     | What to do                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| "Confirming your setup with Stripe."         | myK9Show is waiting to hear back from Stripe. Usually a few seconds.              | Wait. If it lingers, choose **Check again**. |
| **Under review by Stripe**                   | Stripe is checking the club's details. This usually finishes within a day or two. | Nothing. Come back tomorrow.                 |
| **Payouts enabled** (green)                  | You are done. Your club will be paid after each show.                             | Nothing, ever.                               |
| "Your setup with Stripe isn't finished yet." | You left Stripe before the last screen.                                           | Choose **Finish setting up**.                |

You can stop partway and come back later. Nothing you typed is lost.

**Do this well before your show opens for entries.** A show cannot take card payments until the
box says **Payouts enabled**, and Stripe's review can take a day or two.

## If something does not go smoothly

**"Your Stripe setup link expired before you finished."**
For safety, Stripe's pages stop working after a short time if left open. Nothing was lost. Choose
**Continue to Stripe** and carry on where you stopped.

**It has said "Under review by Stripe" for more than two days.**
Stripe probably wants one more piece of information. Open the Payments tab and choose
**Add missing information**. Stripe will show you exactly what it needs. The two usual requests
are:

- **A photo of your ID.** A picture of your driver's license taken with your phone, front and back.
- **Proof of the club's tax ID.** A photo or scan of the IRS letter that shows the club's name and
  EIN. If you cannot find it, the IRS will send a replacement (ask for "letter 147C") if you call
  them.

**Stripe says the club's name and tax ID do not match.**
This is the legal-name problem described above. Choose **Add missing information**, correct the
name so it matches the IRS letter exactly, and submit again.

**I entered the wrong bank account.**
After you are set up, sign in to Stripe at **express.stripe.com** with the email and phone you
used, open payout settings, add the correct account and make it the default. If you have not
finished setting up yet, choose **Finish setting up** and correct it there.

**I never got the text message.**
Check that you typed your mobile number correctly, then ask Stripe to send it again. A landline
cannot receive it.

**I do not see a Payments tab.**
You are not yet an admin of your club in myK9Show. Email us at the address below.

## Being paid

**When.** Three days after your show's last day. Your bank usually shows the deposit the next
business day. Weekends and bank holidays can add a day.

**How much.**

> **Online entry fees collected, minus any refunds your secretary issued.**

**Example:** 40 entries at $32 each is $1,280 in entry fees. One entry is withdrawn and your
secretary refunds its $32. Your club receives **$1,248**.

Entries paid by check or cash at the show are not part of the deposit. Your club already has that
money.

**How you will know.** Stripe emails you when a deposit is sent. In myK9Show, the Payments tab
lists each show, what was collected, what was refunded, and what was paid to the club.

**For your treasurer's report.** Your show secretary can print the show's Financial Report from
the show's Reports tab. It lists every entry and what was paid.

## Common questions

**Does the club pay anything?**
No. Not to myK9Show, and not to Stripe. The service fee is paid by exhibitors at checkout and never
comes out of your club's entry fees.

**What if we need to refund an entry?**
Your show secretary refunds it from the entry in myK9Show, and the money goes back to the
exhibitor's card. This works up until your club is paid, three days after the show. The service fee
is not refunded.

**What if we need to refund someone after we have been paid?**
By then the money is in your club's bank account, so your club pays the exhibitor directly, by
check or however you prefer. myK9Show cannot send a card refund once the club has been paid.

**What if the deposit does not arrive?**
We are alerted automatically if a payment to a club fails. The most common cause is that recent
card payments have not finished clearing, and it is retried automatically the next night. If you
have not been paid within a week of your show's last day, email us.

**Can we change our bank account later?**
Yes. Sign in at **express.stripe.com**, open payout settings, add the new account and make it the
default. The next deposit goes there.

**What happens when we get a new treasurer?**
Email us before the handover if you can. The account belongs to the club, not to the person, but
Stripe needs to know who the new responsible person is, and the sign-in email and phone number
need to change to theirs.

**Is my personal information safe?**
You enter it on Stripe's pages, not ours. myK9Show never receives your date of birth, home address
or Social Security number.

**Do I need to sign in to Stripe regularly?**
No. After setup you never need to. You can sign in at **express.stripe.com** if you want to see
past deposits.
