The Vision

One platform, from entry to ribbon

myK9Show is the companion to the scoring app you already know. Where myK9Q handles the ring, myK9Show handles everything around it — entries, payments, show management, results, and the exhibitor experience.

What you've been asking for is being built. Online entries, financial tracking, club management, exhibitor self-service — we're working toward a single system. No integrations to manage. No separate vendor. One platform that knows your shows, your dogs, and your people.

📝

Online Entries

Exhibitors enter shows from their phone in under 30 seconds. Dog info pre-fills. Fees are clear. Done.

💰

Integrated Payments

Stripe-powered checkout with per-entry fees, automatic receipts, and full financial tracking for clubs.

📊

Real-Time Results

As judges score in myK9Q, results appear instantly for exhibitors. No waiting for paper scorecards.

📷

Works Offline

Show day Wi-Fi is unreliable. The platform works without it and syncs automatically when reconnected.

🐾 For Exhibitors

Entering a show should take seconds, not minutes

The exhibitor experience is designed around one principle: respect their time. Pre-filled forms, smart defaults, and a mobile-first interface mean exhibitors spend less time on logistics and more time with their dogs.

1

Browse & discover shows

Filter by date, location, sport, or class level. See what's open, what's filling up, and what's closed — in real time.

2

Add entries to your cart

Select your dog, pick your classes, review fees. The system remembers your dogs and pre-fills registration numbers, call names, and breed info.

3

Checkout with Stripe

Secure, PCI-compliant payment. Entry fees are itemized with a transparent platform fee. Automatic receipt emailed to you.

4

Track everything from your dashboard

View upcoming entries, past results, and payment history. Your personal exhibitor dashboard shows the full picture at a glance.

5

See results as they happen

No more waiting by the ring. As judges score in myK9Q, results flow into your dashboard automatically.

Smart waitlists planned. If a class fills up, exhibitors would land on the waitlist automatically. When a spot opens, the next person gets an offer. If they don't respond, it moves to the next — hands-free for secretaries.

📋 For Trial Secretaries

Day-of operations, without the chaos

Secretaries already love myK9Q at ringside. myK9Show is being built to give them the same streamlined experience for everything before and after the ring — entries, armbands, waitlists, scratches, and financials.

⚡

One-Tap Operations

Scratch an entry, move someone up from the waitlist, assign armbands — each one a single tap. No confirmation dialogs for routine actions.

📑

Automatic Waitlist Management

When someone scratches, the next person on the waitlist gets an offer automatically. 24-hour expiration. Zero secretary intervention needed.

💲

Financial Dashboard

See entry fees collected, pending payments, revenue per class, and payout summaries. All the financial visibility you've been wanting.

📈

Bulk Result Entry

Import or manually enter results in bulk after a trial. Publish per-class, per-trial, or per-show — you control the timing.

🏆 For Clubs

Show creation and revenue tracking for clubs

We're building club tools for show management — templates, judge assignment, fee configuration, and financial oversight — so clubs can move away from spreadsheets.

🛠

Show Creation Wizard

Step-by-step guided setup. Configure trials, classes, levels, fees, and judges. Clone previous shows to save time.

💰

Revenue Tracking

Entry fees flow directly through Stripe. Track revenue per show, per class, and per trial. Transparent platform fee of 3% per entry.

👥

Team Management

Invite trial secretaries, assign permissions, manage who can see what. Role-based access controls keep things clean.

📅

Templates & Cloning

Created a show format that works? Clone it. Reuse class structures, fee schedules, and judge assignments across events.

No upfront licensing fees for clubs. The platform charges a small per-entry fee (3%) instead of expensive annual licenses. You pay when exhibitors pay. If no one enters, it costs you nothing.

Sports

Multiple organizations. Sport-specific scoring.

Each sport gets its own optimized scoresheet — not a generic form builder. The scoring engine understands pass/fail, timed events, and fault deductions natively.

🔐 Reliability

Built for the realities of show day

Dog shows happen in fields, barns, and convention centers — places where Wi-Fi is a suggestion, not a guarantee. The entire platform is designed around that reality.

🔌

Offline-First Architecture

Every critical operation works without internet. Scores, entries, and updates are cached locally and sync automatically when connectivity returns.

🔃

Background Sync

No "syncing..." spinners. No error popups. Changes flow silently in the background. The technology is invisible — by design.

🔒

Security Built In

Row-level security on every database query. Role-based permissions. Audit logging. Data isolation between shows.

📱

Works on Any Device

Progressive Web App — install it like a native app from your browser. No app store. Optimized for tablets at ringside and phones in the field.

They share the same database. When a judge scores an entry in myK9Q, the exhibitor sees that result in myK9Show. When a secretary processes an entry in myK9Show, it appears on the judge's run order in myK9Q. No CSV exports. No manual syncing. It just works.
