# Emergency trial packet

The emergency trial packet is the paper fallback for running a show when the laptop, app, session, or local data is unavailable. It is a snapshot, not live data.

## Before the show

1. Open the show's **Reports** page with **All Trials** and **All Classes** selected.
2. Under **Emergency Trial Packet**, choose **Prepare and email packet**.
3. Open the emailed private link from a device that does not depend on the show laptop.
4. Print the complete packet. Confirm every trial, class, and entry is present.
5. Put the packet in the physical trial box with pens, blank paper, the registry rulebook, and required official forms.
6. In Reports, choose **Mark packet printed** only after the paper is physically in hand.

Email delivery is not readiness. A packet that exists only on the show laptop or in its browser cannot recover a dead device.

## Running on paper

- Use the generation time and `SNAPSHOT — NOT LIVE` footer on every page. If several packets exist, use the newest complete copy.
- Keep each page with the trial day, ring, and class printed in its footer.
- Write check-ins, pulls, move-ups, absences, running-order changes, scores, faults, times, placements, corrections, and incidents directly on the packet.
- Initial corrections. Do not erase the original value so the change remains auditable.
- Have judges and the secretary sign the packet where required. Attach registry-specific official paperwork printed from Reports.
- Preserve every page until results are entered into myK9, checked, released, and submitted.

## Transcribing results back into myK9

1. Gather the entire packet and separate it from older snapshots by generation time.
2. Apply pulls, move-ups, absences, and class-order changes before entering scores.
3. Enter each paper score by armband and class.
4. Have a second person read back armband, result, time, faults, and placement from the paper while the first person checks myK9.
5. Compare class totals and judge/secretary certifications before releasing or submitting results.
6. Record who transcribed and checked the results, the completion time, and how discrepancies were resolved.
7. Retain the paper packet with the club's trial records according to the applicable registry rules.

## Acceptance drill

Before relying on this at a real trial, a human must run a mock trial day using only the printed packet, with myK9 unavailable. Record anything essential that is missing, correct the packet, and repeat the transcription check. MYK9-198 is not complete until this drill and a signed-out email-link print test are recorded.
