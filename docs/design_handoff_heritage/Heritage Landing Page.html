§ VI — On the Day

Order of the Day

A flow, not a timetable
check-in › briefing › trials › lunch › awards
Doors open at 7:00 AM each day. Order of classes set the morning of, dependent on entry count.
i.
Hospitality
Provided on site, all three days
Coffee and breakfast tacos at check-in. Lunch and refreshments served midday. Compliments of the club — bring a chair.
ii.
Awards & Honours
Presented at the close of each trial
Hand-tied rosettes for first through fourth in every class. Qualifying ribbons for all qualifying scores. Engraved bowl for High in Trial each day. Silver platter for High Combined Master at weekend's end.
iii.
The Venue
Live Oak Civic Center
Indoor, climate-controlled, sealed concrete floors. Crating in the adjacent ballroom. Ample free parking. Soft-sided crates only.
iv.
House Rules
For the comfort & safety of all
No flexi-leads on the trial floor. Bitches in season welcome — notify secretary at check-in. Vaccination records may be requested. Pick up after your dog.
