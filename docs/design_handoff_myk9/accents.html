Color governance

Brand identityAlways terracotta. Logo, hero, marketing, emails, app icon. Never user-overridable.

Live / judgingAlways ring-green (#4e7c53). System-owned; communicates "a judge is scoring right now."
Warnings / errorsSystem palette — amber #c88b1a, red #b04835. Never follows accent.
PlacementsAKC-standard rosette colors: blue 1st, red 2nd, yellow 3rd, white 4th. Convention, not theme.
