v1 vs v2

Same product, two directions. v1 plays it safe; v2 commits to a terracotta-led, rosette-signed brand with Fraunces instead of Playfair.

v1 · what we had

  • Teal + terracotta — two accent colors, not a clear primary
  • Playfair Display display serif — competent, but on every "elegant SaaS"
  • No domain motif — could be any booking app
  • Apple-generic mobile — scoring felt like logging a form
  • Gradient show covers in blue/pink/purple — disconnected palette

v2 · what changed

  • Terracotta leads everywhere; ring-green reserved for "judging live"
  • Fraunces (opsz + SOFT) — italics get genuine personality
  • Rosette motif in logo, hero, placement pills, score background
  • Theatrical in-ring state — LIVE banner, serif dog name, warm score card
  • Earth-toned covers — terracotta, clay, sage, slate — one family
