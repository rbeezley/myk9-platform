# SMS Ring Alerts

**Last Updated: August 16, 2026**

> **Ring alerts are not available yet.** Text alerts have not launched, there is currently no way to sign up for them, and no messages are being sent. This page describes the program as it will operate when they do.

myK9Show can send you a text message when your dog is getting close to the ring, so you can be ready to run instead of watching the run order.

This page describes that program in full. It exists so anyone — an exhibitor deciding whether to sign up, or a mobile carrier reviewing our messaging program — can see exactly what we send, how you agree to receive it, and how you stop it.

---

## What the program is

**Program name:** myK9Show Ring Alerts

**What we send:** one text when your dog is approaching the ring. That is the entire program. We do not send marketing, promotional offers, results, or schedule changes by SMS.

**How often:** message frequency varies. It depends on how many dogs you have entered and how many classes they are running — typically a handful of messages per trial day, and none at all on days you are not competing.

**Cost:** myK9Show does not charge for ring alerts. **Message and data rates may apply** from your mobile carrier.

**Carriers:** supported on major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Example messages:

- `Cooper (#314) is 3 dogs out in Excellent Interiors - myK9Show`
- `Cooper is up next in Novice - myK9Show`

## How you opt in

Ring alerts are **off by default**. Nothing is ever sent to you unless you turn them on yourself.

To opt in, sign in and go to **Account → Notification Settings**, enter your mobile number, and tick the consent box. The box is never pre-checked, and it is separate from every other agreement — consenting to ring alerts is not bundled with creating an account, entering a show, or paying an entry fee.

The consent box reads:

> Text me when my dog is close to the ring. By checking this box I agree to receive SMS ring alerts from myK9Show at the number above. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

**SMS alerts are optional and are never required.** You can enter shows, pay entry fees, and use every part of myK9Show without providing a mobile number. Declining ring alerts has no effect on your entries.

When you opt in, we record the number you consented with, the date and time, and the exact wording shown to you above.

**Consent belongs to the phone number, not to your account.** If you change your mobile number, the new number has not agreed to anything — you will need to opt in again on the new number.

---

## How you opt out

**Reply STOP to any message.** That ends all ring alerts to that number immediately. We also accept STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT.

You can also turn ring alerts off at any time in **Account → Notification Settings**, without sending a text.

Opting out affects text messages only. Your entries, results, and account are untouched, and you will still receive the email notifications you have enabled.

How you start again depends on how you stopped:

- **If you replied STOP**, text **START** from the same number. That is the only way to resume alerts — your opt-out is held by our messaging provider at the network level, so switching the setting back on in your account will not clear it.
- **If you turned alerts off in your notification settings** and never texted STOP, simply turn them back on there.

Your notification settings show the number to text.

The only party that receives your number is the SMS delivery provider that transmits the messages you asked for, acting on our behalf and under contract.
