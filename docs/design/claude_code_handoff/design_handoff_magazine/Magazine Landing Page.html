Letter from the chair

A welcome to all entrants

On behalf of the officers and members of the Bexar County Kennel Club, our second running of the AKC Scent Work programme at Live Oak.

For seventy-nine years our club has put on shows that exhibitors look forward to. This spring we return with three days of scent work — a discipline built on partnership, patience, and the impossibly good nose of a willing dog.

Three days, two judges, six trials. Mrs. Beagles judges Containers and Interiors in Hall A. Mr. Whitfield judges Exteriors, Buried, and Handler Discrimination in Hall B. We have set the entry limit at 360 runs to keep the pace humane for both dogs and judges, and we expect to fill — last year we closed nine days early.

Coffee and breakfast tacos at check-in. Lunch on site, all three days, prepared by the hospitality committee. Crating in the adjacent ballroom, free of charge. Free parking. We are a small club, and the volunteers running this trial are the same people you will see ribboning in the morning and stewarding in the afternoon. Please treat them kindly.

We look forward to seeing you and your dogs at Live Oak.

Sarah Whitman
Trial Chair · BCKC

The bench

Two judges, six trials

Both approved by the American Kennel Club; each handling three of the six trials on a fixed rotation published in advance.

Plate I CB
Trials i · iii · v

Mrs. Catherine Beagles

of Austin, Texas

AKC judge since 2014. Approved for all Scent Work elements at all levels. Former trial chair of Lone Star Working Dog Club. Lives in Austin with two terriers, both retired competitors. Sees the dogs as the protagonists.

Plate II MW
Trials ii · iv · vi

Mr. Marcus Whitfield

of Boulder, Colorado

AKC judge since 2017. Approved through Master and Detective. Detection-dog handler, twenty years. Author of Reading the Wind: A Practical Guide to Exterior Searches (Two Coast Press, 2023). Patient with handlers; ruthless with hides.

On the day

What to expect

Doors open at seven; first class at eight-thirty; approximate finish at five-thirty. The day flows like clockwork because we want it to.

  • Hand-tied rosettes for first through fourth in every class
  • Qualifying ribbons for all qualifying scores
  • Engraved bowl for High in Trial — presented each day
  • Silver platter for High Combined Master — presented Sunday evening
  • New Title certificates presented at end of trial
  • Lunch and refreshments on site Friday, Saturday, and Sunday
