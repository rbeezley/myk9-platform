§01

Welcome & purpose

~280 WORDS · CHAIR'S NOTE

This document is the official reference for the Bexar County Kennel Club's Spring Scent Work Trial, held 12–14 June 2026 at the Live Oak Civic Center, San Antonio. Use it before entry to confirm levels, fees, and dates; use it on site to find the venue, the vet, and the schedule.

The trial is AKC licensed (§2026-2841). Six trials over three days. Two judges. All four elements plus Handler Discrimination and Detective at Master. Entry limit is 360 runs · firm; over-entries are refunded.

The trial committee is a small volunteer body. Most volunteers will be running the trial in the morning and stewarding in the afternoon. Please be patient with us and pick up after your dog.

Sarah Whitman · TRIAL CHAIR · sarah@bckc.org
