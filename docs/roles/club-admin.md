# Role: Club Admin

## Scope for fall 2026
**Functional only.** Club Admin exists as a data model and a minimal "assign my secretaries" screen. It is not a polished dashboard home. Most real work still happens through the Secretary role. A full Club Admin experience is post-fall.

## Who they are
A club officer (often the club secretary, president, or event chair) who is accountable to the club for its shows happening smoothly. In the fall target audience, this is frequently the *same person* as the show Secretary — the two roles are only separate when a club has multiple secretaries, or has officers who aren't themselves running shows.

## Must accomplish (fall 2026)

1. **Create and maintain the club profile** — club name, AKC/UKC/ASCA club numbers, mailing address, contact info. This data flows into every show the club hosts and every AKC results submission.
2. **Assign secretaries to the club's shows** — when a show is created for this club, pick which secretary account owns it.
3. **View the club's shows** — a read-only list of shows this club is hosting or has hosted. Not a dashboard.

## Should never have to think about
- Creating their own club from scratch — site admin creates clubs from the landing-page request form, then hands the first admin seat over.
- Who owns a specific show — assignment is explicit and visible.
- Stale contact info flowing into AKC submissions — profile updates propagate forward.

## Deferred to post-fall
- Club membership roster.
- Club training classes and student tracking.
- Club-wide dashboards and analytics.
- Self-service club creation (bypassing site-admin review).
- Multiple admins per club.
