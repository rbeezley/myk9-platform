# Offline desk payments: cash and check recorded with no connection (MYK9-1936 step 3)

> **Status:** Active

Linear: MYK9-1936, step 3. Builds on step 1 (#4446, every desk payment carries a client payment id) and step 2 (MYK9-1930, #4158, a desk late entry gets its entry form when it syncs). Design only. App paths are under `apps/myk9show/src/`; queue files (`MutationManager.ts`, `mutation-*.ts`, `MutationUploadRunner.ts`, `types.ts`, `core/`) are under `packages/replication/src/`; migrations are under `supabase/migrations/`.

**Goal.** With no connection, or with venue Wi-Fi that reaches nothing, the secretary records cash or a check on an entry form as normal. It shows as waiting to send, counts in the form's balance, and is sent on its own. A refusal is shown with its reason. Close-out says what has not been sent. **Not included:** card payments at the table, refunds, "Payment Due", "Paid in Full: Online", and any change to what the Financial Report counts.

## 1. What exists, and why

- **The write is online and not optimistic, on purpose** (`hooks/useEnrollmentLedgerActions.ts:160-161`, MYK9-677, `b8c0f35617`): the server computes status and paid total, and the form is patched only from its answer (`:179-186`). A lost answer reads `LEDGER_UNCONFIRMED` (`:71-72`). `services/database/show-payments.ts:9-20` records the same choice; the late-entry wizard's money rides on the entry row (`features/registration/submitOfflineLateEntry.ts:179-191`).
- **Offline, the form has no balance.** Enrollment money is read online only (`services/database/entries/secretaryReadReplication.ts:185-208`; offline throws at `:147`), so the form reads "Amount not available" (`components/entries/management/enrollmentPayment.ts:209-226`, MYK9-1968). There is no `enrollments` replica.
- **The server dedupes on the id, in a set order.** The wrapper checks the form exists and who may record (`20261126061937_myk9_1936_payment_id_same_date_and_reference.sql:39-48`), then a changed date or check number (`:50-71`). The core then answers an id it already holds, or refuses an id reused for another form, method or amount (`20261114013900_myk9_1961_refunded_fee_not_owed.sql:442-459`). So a replay of a payment that landed can still be refused, for a reason that arose later (role revoked, form deleted). A NULL amount means "what is due now" (`:496-497`). An explicit amount over the due is accepted, as money on account.
- **The queue.** `queueMutation` takes an `rpc` with exact `args` (`MutationManager.ts:167-208`, `types.ts:230-245`); `prepareMutation` lets a caller commit a local row and its queue row in one transaction (`MutationManager.ts:230-262`, MYK9-1471). An RPC `INSERT` result is discarded (`mutation-execute.ts:104-135`). The acknowledgement marks the local row synced (`mutation-row-sync.ts:22-79`) before the queue row is deleted (`MutationUploadRunner.ts:318-329`). A mutation whose dependency is in the dead letters waits (`MutationUploadRunner.ts:239-252`).
- **Retry.** A transport failure spares the retry budget only when the uplink probe says the server is down (`mutation-retry.ts:41-61`). A timeout while the probe says "up" spends it, so an attempt that committed but whose answer was lost can end as a `max-retries` dead letter. `42501` is permanent; `22023` is retryable unless the RPC is listed in `RPC_PERMANENT_CODES` (`mutation-utils.ts:272-274`). Direct write RPCs have no shared deadline (`features/at-show/staleClass/completeStaleClassRpc.ts:10-17`).
- **Already counted:** every non-score queue row and dead letter is in the device's unsent count and the sign-out guard (`services/replication/unsentWork.ts:229-252`).

## 2. The shape proposed

**The local payment record.** Each payment saved without a confirmed answer is a row on the device, keyed by its client payment id. It is written in the same transaction as its queue row (`prepareMutation`), so neither exists without the other. It holds the show, the form id (or the entry id, below), the exhibitor's name, amount, method, date, check number and `firstSentAt` (when its first send began). The acknowledgement marks it sent in the same step that settles the queue, so a reload never loses the fact. PR 3 checks that a replication-store table can be registered with no download; if not, it adds one store to the same database. The queue row is table `show_payments`, `INSERT`, row id = the client payment id, `rpc.args` explicit, no `dependsOn` between payments.

**When it queues.** Every cash or check save sends the amount she was shown, online too ("Paid in Full" stops sending NULL). The direct call and any later replay therefore name the same payment, so the dedupe answers instead of refusing. A save goes to the queue under its id:

- at once, when the browser reports offline;
- when the direct call fails at the transport level, or gives no answer within 10 seconds (the precedent is 12 s, `completeStaleClassRpc.ts:17`). This covers venue Wi-Fi with no internet, where `navigator.onLine` stays true. If the abandoned call lands after all, the replay gets the dedupe's answer.

The dialog then closes, reading "Waiting to send. It will be sent on its own." (the existing wording, `features/at-show/scoresNotSent/unsentScoreReason.ts:64`). A refusal that comes back inside the wait stays in the dialog as today. Refunds, "Payment Due" and "Paid in Full: Online" stay online, and say "Needs a connection."

**A late entry taken before its first sync (P1).** The key is the entry's own id: the device generates it (`submitOfflineLateEntry.ts:286`) and the server keeps it. A payment on a form that exists only on the device is queued to a new RPC, `record_entry_form_payment(p_entry_id, …)`, with `dependsOn` the entry's insert (`ReplicatedTable.getPendingMutationIdsForRow`, `core/ReplicatedTable.ts:275`). It therefore sends only after the entry lands, and by then the attach trigger has set the entry's form (`20261119173700_myk9_1930_late_entry_gets_form.sql:393-450`). The RPC reads `entries.registration_id` and calls `record_enrollment_payment` with it, so the dedupe, guard and permission are the same. With no form (the trigger skips a caller who cannot manage the show, or a dog with no owner, `:403-422`) it refuses: "entry % has no entry form". Any entry on the form will do, because the trigger joins the dog owner's form for the show (`:418-425`). If the entry itself is refused, the payment never sends; it reads "Waiting on the entry, which could not be sent" and may be removed, since it never left the device. This replaces `SAVED_ON_DEVICE_PAYMENT_NOTE` for payments (`enrollmentPayment.ts:201-202`).

**The server guard (migration, PR 2).** New `p_refuse_over_due` (default false), set only on queued sends. It refuses a payment larger than what the form now owes: `payment % is more than the % this form now owes`. The check runs after the dedupe (`:442-459`) and after the due (`:489-497`), so a landed replay is answered. This covers "another device recorded it", "refunded or comped meanwhile" and over-balance. The same migration adds `record_entry_form_payment`, drops and recreates the changed signature, and restates grants (`20261126061937…:83-86`). In the app, `RPC_PERMANENT_CODES` lists `22023` for both RPCs, and the dead letter keeps the Postgres code.

**States: refused is proven, never assumed (P1).**

| State       | When                                                                                         | She sees and may do                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Waiting     | Queued, no answer                                                                            | "Waiting to send." Nothing to do.                                                                                                                                  |
| Sent        | Acknowledged, or a ledger read lists its id                                                  | "Sent", until it retires (below).                                                                                                                                  |
| Not checked | A `max-retries` dead letter; or a coded refusal not yet checked against the ledger           | "Not confirmed. This payment may already be recorded." **Check again** reads the ledger by id. Never Remove.                                                       |
| Refused     | A coded refusal (`22023`, `42501`) **and** a ledger read begun after it does not list the id | "Could not be sent: <reason>." **Remove**; and "If you took this money, record it again." A new id is safe here only because the ledger proved the old one absent. |

Once **Check again** finds the id absent, it offers **Try again**, which re-queues the same mutation and id (`retryFailedMutation`, `MutationManager.ts:398`). Reasons: over the due, "Another payment was recorded on this form while this one waited."; `42501`, "You do not have permission to record payments for this show."; a future date, the existing friendly text (`useEnrollmentLedgerActions.ts:81-87`); no form, "This entry form no longer exists."; anything else, "Something went wrong with this payment." The ledger read adds `client_payment_id` to `LEDGER_COLUMNS` (`show-payments.ts:59`). Authenticated users hold SELECT on the table (`20260925181937…:148`); column grants are not checked.

**The balance, and retiring the overlay (P1).** PR 1 puts form money on the device: a read-only, show-scoped `enrollments` replica, never written locally. Each row is stamped with when the pull that wrote it began. Local payments are an overlay, never written into that row. The form reads `$50.00 · $25.00 received · $25.00 waiting to send · $0.00 due`, and the badge stays the server's. Against the shown snapshot, a local payment:

- **counts** if the snapshot's read began before the payment's `firstSentAt`, or if a ledger read begun after the snapshot arrived does not list its id;
- **retires** (the local row is deleted) if a ledger read lists its id and the snapshot's read began after that ledger read ended;
- otherwise leaves the due reading "Updating" with no figure. The refresh after any send reads the ledger, then the form.

Both orders, a reload, and a commit whose answer was lost resolve the same way, with no double count and no gap.

**A $0 Paid in Full (P2).** With local payments, "Paid in Full" offers the due less the overlay. When that is not positive, the dialog reads "Nothing is due on this form." and confirm is disabled. The server would refuse a $0 amount (`20261114013900…:501-503`).

**Where she resolves it (P2).** One list, the close-out card's "Payments received" group. It is the cash-box home ("Check the cash box against the payments below", `features/show-workbench/ShowCloseoutSummary.tsx:116`). The group lists every local payment not yet retired, each with its state and actions. Each row names the exhibitor from the local record, so a payment whose form is gone still shows and can be removed there. Each row also has **Open form**, which uses the existing `?registration=` / `?entry=` deep link (`components/entries/management/entryManagementCockpitParams.ts:136-147`) and is hidden when the form is gone. The form's Payment section shows the same rows for its own payments. Show Desk gets an "N payments not sent" pill only while any row is Not checked or Refused, linking to the close step. The existing Finish-list link is used by two pills (`features/show-map/showDeskPendingSignals.ts:226,243`); check that it reaches `pages/secretary/ShowCloseStep.tsx`. It is not the "Resolve N payments" pill, whose `payment: 'pending'` filter (`:196-206`) drops a form that is now paid. No new page or filter.

**Close-out and the Financial Report.** Neither changes what it counts. Close-out takes its total from the ledger read (`ShowCloseoutSummary.tsx:49-72`), and the reconciliation above uses that same read. So "2 payments ($50.00) on this device are not in this total" is true by construction: a payment the read lists is Sent, not in the line. Offline there is no total, and the line says only "waiting to send". The chip is not "Ready" while any row is unretired. The Financial Report counts entry rows, not this queue, so it cannot promise inclusion either way. Its line, beside the existing warning (`components/reports/FinancialReport.tsx:46`), reads: "2 payments on this device ($50.00) have not been confirmed as sent. This report counts only what the server has recorded." On upload, invalidate `['show-payments']` explicitly; the generic handler invalidates `[tableName]` (`providers/ReplicationSyncProvider.tsx:729-731`).

**Identity and permission.** Queue only when the queue owner resolves (`mutation-identity.ts:55-69`). Otherwise the form says "No connection. Write the payment down and record it here when you're back online." The server decides at replay. A different account signing in holds the row (`MutationUploadRunner.ts:309-315`). Sign-out already warns about it.

## 3. Do nothing, delete it, or link to what exists

Keep it online, and say plainly offline: "No connection. Write the payment down and record it here when you're back online." It costs one small PR with no risk. But the owner rejected "only tell her she is offline". It puts a paper ledger back on show day (`docs/goals/fall-2026-launch-readiness.md:71` asks for "offline-tolerant, and hard to mess up"). Close-out would not know about the paper. And venue Wi-Fi with no internet would still end in "could not confirm". Not the answer. The note stays for what remains online and for an unresolved identity.

## 4. Sizing and split

Money work and migrations do not batch.

1. **Form money on the device.** Read-only `enrollments` replica with a per-row pull-start stamp. Done when: a cold offline reload shows each synced form's total, received and due. Before building, check that every writer of enrollment money moves `updated_at` (the core does, `20261114013900…:532`; card writers are unchecked) and that `enrollments_select` admits the pull.
2. **Server.** `p_refuse_over_due`, `record_entry_form_payment`, grants, SQL tests, friendly refusal text. `supabase db push` before PR 3 merges.
3. **Record and send.** The local record, the queue, the offline and unreachable handoff, explicit amounts, permanent codes, the four states with ledger reconciliation, and the late-entry key. Done when: criteria 2 and 5 hold, and no path offers a new id for an unproven refusal.
4. **On the form.** The overlay, retirement, "Updating", the $0 rule, and the Payment-section rows. Done when: criteria 1 and 3 hold.
5. **Close-out list, Show Desk pill, report line.** Done when: criterion 4 holds online and offline, and the report counts what it did before.

## 5. Testing

- **Unit.** Assertion-first on the queued call: `toHaveBeenCalledWith('show_payments', 'INSERT', id, …, { name: 'record_enrollment_payment', args: expect.objectContaining({ p_client_payment_id: id, p_amount: 25, p_refuse_over_due: true }) })`, and the entry-keyed variant with `dependsOn`. The unreachable server: `navigator.onLine` true, the RPC never answers (fake timers) or throws `TypeError`, so the save is queued with the same id and the same amount. States: a `max-retries` dead letter is Not checked, with no Remove; a `42501` whose id the ledger lists is Sent; Refused only after a ledger read misses the id. Overlay: ledger before the form pull, after it, and a reload between them; the $0 Paid in Full is disabled.
- **SQL** (`supabase/tests/`, CI only): the guard refuses over the due; a landed id replayed after the form is paid is answered; unflagged callers are unchanged; the entry RPC resolves the form, or refuses an entry with none. Grants are checked on the applied database.
- **Replay twice.** The first send commits, then throws a transport error; the second gets the answer. One ledger row, an empty queue, no dead letter.
- **E2e** (one spec, a row in `docs/qa/e2e-suite-map.md`, a seeded form with money due checked in `supabase/seed-demo.sql`). Offline: record cash, see Waiting and the lower due, then go online and see it retire with one ledger row. Online but unreachable: abort the RPC by route interception and see Waiting, not "could not confirm". Refused: answer with a `22023` body by route interception, never by writing to the shared database.
