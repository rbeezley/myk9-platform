Letter from the chair

A welcome to all entrants

Dear exhibitor — on behalf of the officers and members of the Bexar County Kennel Club, it is my pleasure to welcome you to our seventy-ninth licensed trial weekend, and our second running the AKC Scent Work programme at this venue.

Three days, two judges, six trials. Containers and Interiors with Mrs. Beagles in Hall A; Exteriors and Buried with Mr. Whitfield in Hall B. The order of running publishes one week prior. We have set the entry limit at 360 runs to keep the pace humane for both dogs and judges, and we expect to fill.

We are a small club. The volunteers running this trial are the same people you will see ribboning in the morning and stewarding in the afternoon. Treat them kindly.

Sarah Whitman
Trial Chair
