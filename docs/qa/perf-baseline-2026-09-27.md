## Initial findings

- 91/206 measured LCP samples exceeded 2.5 s. This lab comparison is a warning signal; formal Core Web Vitals pass/fail requires field data at the 75th percentile.
- 91/176 mobile samples exceeded the custom time-to-usable target for their profile.
- TBT proxy ranged 43–379 ms; the lab target is <200 ms. This sums observed long tasks through 500 ms after readiness.
- Maximum observed JavaScript transfer was 1.65 MB. The largest emitted minified chunk was index-BZdJTEpB.js (3.53 MB). Initial JS delivery and parse are candidates for follow-up profiling, not established root causes.
- The largest entry chunk transferred in 103/103 measured cold attempts, at 1123152–1123152 bytes over the wire. Its transfer is a shared cost across those routes.
- The slowest measured secretary or ringside cold route on slow 4G was /shows/dededede-0000-0000-0000-000000000010/show-day: 19147 ms median across 3 runs.
- The largest observed resource request count was 2753 on /cart (fast-4g-mobile, cold); investigate the request mix before assigning a cause.
- No usable measurement was obtained for secretary-reports, admin-users. These routes need reliability investigation before they can be included in speed rankings.
- Local preview TTFB ranged 1–10 ms; this is not representative of production server response time.
