# Premium Website Checklist

Actionable audit checklist for building high-trust websites. Adapted from design principles — apply when reviewing or building myK9 Platform UI.

---

## 1. Foundation: Intentional First Impression

- [ ] Visitors immediately understand what you do, who it's for, and why it matters — without scrolling
- [ ] Nothing looks templated, rushed, or thrown together — even simple layouts feel deliberate
- [ ] Professional tone, confident messaging, and visual clarity prevent the "this feels sketchy" reaction

---

## 2. Bespoke Assets & Graphics

- [ ] Visual elements are designed for the brand — not pulled from generic icon libraries
- [ ] Graphics guide attention, break up content, and aid comprehension
- [ ] Every visual matches the tone, colour palette, and personality of the brand
- [ ] Nothing is decorative "just because" — every asset earns its place

---

## 3. Brand Strategy

### Logo

- [ ] Logo looks considered and credible (simple is fine, amateur isn't)

### Colour Palette

- [ ] Cohesive colour system — colours create a consistent mood, not visual chaos
- [ ] Restraint over variety — fewer colours used with confidence feels more premium

### Typography

- [ ] Fonts elevate the brand rather than cheapen it
- [ ] Readability comes first — no outdated, childish, or hard-to-read fonts
- [ ] Typography is one of the fastest signals of quality — treat it accordingly

---

## 4. Subtle Animation

- [ ] Scroll-based motion: elements fade or move naturally as users scroll
- [ ] Interactive feedback: buttons and links respond on hover/click
- [ ] No distracting gimmicks — nothing spins, bounces, or steals attention from content
- [ ] Animations guide users, never confuse them (no "rage clicking")

---

## 5. Strategic Structure: Sitemap, UX & CTAs

- [ ] Pages make sense together, not just individually
- [ ] Each page leads naturally to the next step (clear user journey)
- [ ] No decision paralysis — visitors always know where to go next
- [ ] CTAs are obvious, easy to find, and easy to take
- [ ] Page hierarchy, internal linking, and content organisation work for humans and algorithms

---

## 6. Client Autonomy

- [ ] Content can be updated without developer dependency
- [ ] Site isn't fragile, locked down, or intimidating to edit
- [ ] Adding pages, updating services, and refreshing content is straightforward
- [ ] A site that's easy to update stays fresh — freshness is something users feel

---

## 7. Handover & Post-Launch Care

- [ ] Structured handover training for anyone managing the site
- [ ] Clear expectations on what's self-service vs. needs help
- [ ] Post-launch support window for questions and issues
- [ ] Optional ongoing maintenance without pressure or hidden costs

---

## The Litmus Test

> If someone landed on your website for the first time today, would they think "These people are professional — I trust them" or "Something about this feels... off"?

---

## Key Principle

A premium website isn't louder — it's **clearer, calmer, and more intentional**.
