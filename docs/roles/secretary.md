# Role: Secretary

## Who they are
The person who runs a dog show on behalf of a club. Often a retired volunteer who has done this for years on paper or in the mySWT Access app. Computer-savviness is low to moderate. They are the single point of accountability for everything that happens at their show.

## Emotional intent
> "In control of this show."

The secretary should feel that the platform is a tool they command, not a puzzle they solve. Nothing mid-show should surprise them.

## Must accomplish (fall 2026)

1. **Communicate with exhibitors** — send entry confirmations, waitlist notifications, schedule-change alerts, and general announcements from a single outbox.
2. **Create shows, trials, and classes** — set up the full show structure (show → trial → class) per sanctioning body (AKC, UKC, ASCA).
3. **Assign judges to classes** — balance workload and entry counts across assigned judges.
4. **Accept, waitlist, or reject entries** — decide which entries are in, out, or waitlisted.
5. **Manage mail-in entries** — record paper entries that arrive with checks, creating dog and person records when they don't already exist.
6. **Record payments and refunds** — mark mail-in entries paid when checks clear; process refunds for cancelled entries per club policy. Online entries are auto-marked paid via Stripe.
7. **Manage class changes and move-ups** — transfer entries between classes as exhibitors request.
8. **Manage volunteers** — maintain a roster and assign stewards and helpers to specific rings, trials, and time slots. Publish the resulting volunteer schedule alongside the judge schedule and running order.
9. **Publish the running order** — post when each class runs and which ring it uses, so exhibitors can plan their day.
10. **Manage class schedule and status** — start/stop classes, mark them in progress or complete, handle real-time ordering.
11. **Manage scoring** — either input results from paper scoresheets (when myK9Q isn't in use) or verify results against paper scoresheets (when it is).
12. **Run day-of operations** — operate the check-in desk (in myK9Show, not myK9Q), handle no-shows, make real-time class adjustments, coordinate with stewards and hospitality.
13. **Produce and distribute reports** — generate, verify, print, and post all official reports: catalogs, judge reports, certifications, financials, entry counts, waitlists, labels, and the rest of the Phase 2 Reports set.
14. **Close out the show** — submit results to AKC via the XML export, finalize financial reconciliation, archive the show.

## Should never have to think about
- Which "add dog" button to click — every path leads to the same dog-creation flow.
- Whether a change syncs to myK9Q — replicated data is the default.
- Remembering to pay for entries they already paid for — online and mail-in paid entries look identical downstream.
- Manually copying data between screens — the platform passes context between related actions.
- Fighting navigation — their home screen shows their whole job in one place.

## Primary app
**myK9Show.** Day-of check-in desk explicitly lives here so the secretary never has to switch apps during the show. Scoring happens in myK9Q when the club uses it.

## Deferred to post-fall
- Self-service club onboarding (site admin creates clubs for fall).
- Full exhibitor self-service (withdraws, move-ups, pay-later for mail-ins).
- Volunteer day-of attendance tracking and real-time reassignment.
- Multi-secretary collaboration on the same show.
