# Club Treasurer Guide — Receiving Show Payouts

This guide explains how your club receives entry fee revenue through myK9Show. You do not need a
Stripe account before your show is created. You'll be invited to set one up when the time comes.

---

## The short version

myK9Show collects entry fees on your club's behalf during the entry period. Three days after your
show's end date, the funds are automatically transferred to your club's bank account via Stripe.
You don't need to log in anywhere or click anything — it just arrives.

---

## Step 1 — Accept the Stripe Connect invitation

When your club is ready to accept online payments, the platform admin will send you a setup link.
The link opens Stripe's Express onboarding flow. It takes about 10 minutes.

**What you'll need:**

- Your club's legal name (as it appears on your tax documents)
- Your club's EIN (federal tax ID) or, for small clubs filing as individuals, your SSN
- A US bank account and routing number for payouts
- A phone number for two-factor verification

**What Stripe will ask:**

1. Business type (typically "Non-profit" or "Other" for dog clubs)
2. Business address and phone
3. A representative's name and date of birth (the person responsible for the account)
4. Bank account details for direct deposit

Once you finish, your club's Stripe account is linked to myK9Show. You'll receive a confirmation
email from Stripe.

---

## Step 2 — Nothing (your job is done until payout day)

You don't configure anything in myK9Show or Stripe between onboarding and your first payout. The
platform handles collection, refunds, and the transfer.

---

## What a payout looks like

Three days after your show's end date, myK9Show runs a nightly transfer. By the next business day
you'll see a deposit in your linked bank account. The amount is:

> **Total online entry fees collected − any refunds issued by your secretary**

The platform fee (a small percentage that covers myK9Show's operating costs and Stripe's processing
fee) is deducted before the transfer — what lands in your bank is the net after that fee.

**Example:** 40 entries at $32 each = $1,280 collected. After a 3% platform fee and a $32 refund
for one withdrawn entry, your club receives approximately $1,209.

---

## Checking your payment history

Stripe emails your registered address when a deposit is sent. You can also log in to your
club's Stripe Express dashboard at **express.stripe.com** to see all payouts and the associated
transfer details.

The per-entry breakdown (which dogs, which classes, which fees) lives in myK9Show — ask your show
secretary to pull a report from the Entries Management page if you need itemized records for your
treasurer's report.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does the money arrive?**
Three days after the show's end date, the transfer is sent. Your bank typically posts it the
following business day. Weekends and holidays may add one day.

**What if we need to refund an entry after the show?**
Entries are only refundable through myK9Show before the payout is sent (within the three-day
window). After the money reaches your account, late refunds are handled directly by your club
(check at the next show, issue a check, etc.) — myK9Show does not process post-payout refunds.

**What if we don't receive the payment?**
The platform admin receives an automatic alert if a transfer fails. The most common cause is a
temporary delay in card clearing, and the system retries automatically the next night. If you
haven't received funds within a week of your show's end date, contact the platform admin at
richardbeezley1@gmail.com.

**Can we change our bank account?**
Yes. Log in to your Stripe Express dashboard (express.stripe.com), go to Payout settings, and add
the new account. Set it as the default. Changes take effect for the next transfer.

**What if our club's officers change and the treasurer role transfers?**
Contact the platform admin. The Stripe account is linked to the club, not to an individual — the
admin can update the representative contact information.

**Do we pay Stripe separately?**
No. Stripe's processing fees are absorbed by the platform fee — your club receives one net amount
with nothing additional to pay.
