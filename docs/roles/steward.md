# Role: Steward (stub — deferred for fall 2026)

## Scope decision
Steward is **not a primary role** for the fall 2026 launch. Stewards are managed through the Secretary's volunteer duty (roster + assignment) and see their assignments via the published volunteer schedule. A dedicated Steward experience in myK9Show is deferred post-fall.

## What exists for fall
Only what the Secretary's volunteer management requires:
- Stewards exist in the volunteer roster.
- Stewards are assigned to rings, trials, and time slots as part of the volunteer schedule.
- The volunteer schedule is published alongside the judge schedule and running order.

There is no Steward login, home screen, or myK9Show dashboard in the fall scope. In myK9Q, stewards may have day-of operational tooling — that is tracked separately in myK9Q scope.

## Post-fall
A polished Steward experience in myK9Show — viewing assignments, checking in at their ring, tracking their attendance record over time — is future work. Scope to be defined after fall launch.
