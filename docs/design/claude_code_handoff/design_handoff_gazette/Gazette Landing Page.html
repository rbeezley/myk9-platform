Bexar County Kennel Club to Hold Spring Scent Work Trial

Three days, six trials, three‑hundred and sixty runs to be contested at the Live Oak Civic Center under judges Beagles and Whitfield; entries closing 3 June.

By Sarah Whitman, Trial Chair · Special to the Gazette

For seventy-nine years our club has put on shows that exhibitors look forward to. This spring the Bexar County Kennel Club returns to the Live Oak Civic Center with three days of scent work, a discipline that has grown faster than any AKC programme in recent memory.

The trial committee has set the entry limit firmly at 360 runs, with sixty per trial and one hundred and twenty per day. Last year's spring meeting filled by May twenty-fourth; entrants are advised to submit early.

The judging panel

Mrs. Catherine Beagles of Austin will judge Trials I, III, and V — Containers and Interiors — in Hall A. Mrs. Beagles is approved for all elements at all levels; this is her seventh time judging at a BCKC trial.

Mr. Marcus Whitfield of Boulder, Colorado, judges Trials II, IV, and VI in Hall B — Exteriors, Buried, and Handler Discrimination at Master. Author of Reading the Wind: A Practical Guide to Exterior Searches.

Of hospitality & quarter

Coffee and breakfast tacos will be served at check-in each morning. Lunch is provided by the hospitality committee all three days at no additional charge. The club has reserved blocks at the Hampton Inn Live Oak (Code BCKC26, expires May 18) and at the Holiday Inn Express Selma.

Crating is in the ballroom adjacent to the trial floor. Parking is plentiful and free. Soft-sided crates only, please. No flexi-leads on the trial floor.

The committee

This trial is given by a small club. The volunteers running it will, by Friday afternoon, also be ribboning and stewarding. Please be patient with them, and pick up after your dog.
