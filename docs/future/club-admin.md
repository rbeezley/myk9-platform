# Parked: Club Admin Future Features

## Status
**Deferred from fall 2026 role definition.** The Club Admin role for fall is deliberately Functional-scope only: data model, profile, assign secretaries, view the club's shows. Everything below was listed in the original Club Admin brainstorm and struck from the fall role definition during the 2026-04-11 role-definition session.

## Parked feature list
- **Club membership roster** — track members, their roles in the club, and contact info.
- **Club training classes** — manage training classes offered by the club separate from sanctioned trials.
- **Student tracking** — track students enrolled in club training classes, their progress, attendance, and graduation.
- **Club dashboards and analytics** — cross-show reporting: entries trending over years, revenue, judge history, popular classes.
- **Self-service club creation** — allow users to create clubs without site-admin review.
- **Multiple admins per club** — seat hierarchy, role delegation within a club, audit trails for club-level actions.
- **Club-owned content** — club news, announcements, members-only pages, public club profile.

## When to revisit
After fall 2026 launch, once the Secretary and Exhibitor experiences have proved out and real clubs are using the platform. Scope work should start by asking which of these features clubs are actually asking for — not by building the whole list.

## Not in scope
Do not build any of these for fall. Fall Club Admin is a data row and a single screen. Do not add half-implementations behind feature flags.
