# Training and Presentation Materials

Outlines, shot lists, and planning material for club training, slide decks, and demo walkthroughs. **No PowerPoint, PDF, or video assets are created here yet** — this phase produces outlines and planning only. Actual assets are created after the Phase 0 readiness gate passes and the role guides are in `verified` status.

## Diagrams

Workflow flowcharts and architecture diagrams live in [`../diagrams/`](../diagrams/README.md). They are authored in draw.io (desktop CLI) with `.drawio`/JSON source files in the repo and exported as SVG. They are embedded in guides, KB articles, and deck slides from the same source — one draw.io file feeds all three destinations.

---

## Shot List Discipline

Every screenshot and diagram in final training material must:
- Come from the shot list in `screenshot-shot-list.md`
- Use the seeded fixture accounts listed there (never real customer data)
- Be re-verified against the live app before each training use

Screenshots without a shot-list entry are considered provisional and should not appear in published materials.

---

## Leave-Behind Links

Every training session should end with a leave-behind link list the attendee can bookmark:
- Role-specific user guide
- Top 3 KB articles for their role
- Support contact path (email / page / macro)

Leave-behind lists are defined per-deck in `role-based-deck-outlines.md` once outlines are drafted.
